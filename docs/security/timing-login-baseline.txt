Reading this baseline: the difference in means (0.77ms) is roughly 7% of
either group's standard deviation (~10ms) — well inside normal run-to-run
noise, not a distinguishable signal. Both distributions' shape (mean, p50,
p95, stddev) track each other closely across the board, which is what RS-14's
timing surface is supposed to look like: an attacker measuring login latency
cannot tell an existing account from a non-existent one from this data.

A future run whose means diverge by more than roughly one standard deviation,
or whose p95/p99 diverge while means stay close (a tail effect, not just a
shift), is the regression signal worth investigating — most likely a code
path added on one side of the ok/!ok branch in Authenticator.Attempt without
its counterpart on the other.
